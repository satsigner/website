FAQ

I lost my phone. Can you recover my wallet?

No. satsigner is self-custody software, and we never have your keys. Restore your wallet on a new device with your seed phrase, or with your multisig descriptor and enough cosigner keys.

I forgot my PIN.

The PIN encrypts your keys on the device, and nobody can reset it. Delete and reinstall the App, then restore from your seed phrase.

My balance or transactions look wrong.

Pull down to sync the account. If that doesn't help, go to Settings → Network and try a different Electrum or Esplora server. A wrong script type or derivation path at import can also hide funds. Check those in the account settings.

Can I use my own node?

Yes. In Settings → Network, enter your own Electrum or Esplora server. This keeps your address lookups private.

My transaction is stuck.

If you sent it with Replace-By-Fee, open the transaction and bump the fee. You can also spend one of its outputs with a higher fee (CPFP).

Does satsigner collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
