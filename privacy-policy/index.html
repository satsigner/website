Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy describes how the satsigner mobile application (the "App") handles information. satsigner is free, open-source software. Anyone can audit the source code at github.com/satsigner/satsigner.

Summary: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. Your keys and wallet data stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. The App does not send any information to satsigner or its developers. The App contains no tracking technology, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party crash reporters.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device. None of it reaches us:

  • Seed phrases, private keys, and extended public keys. These are encrypted at rest with your PIN and the device's secure storage.
  • Wallet descriptors, addresses, transactions, UTXOs, and labels.
  • App settings, such as server choices, currency, and display preferences.

If you delete the App, this data is deleted too. Keep your own backup of your seed phrase. We cannot recover it for you.

3. Network services you connect to

A Bitcoin wallet has to talk to the network to work. The App connects directly from your device to the services below. It never routes traffic through satsigner servers, and we run none of them. Each operator can see your IP address and the data your request needs. For example, a blockchain server sees which addresses or transactions you look up. You can change most of these in Settings, or point them at your own node to remove the third party.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera: scans QR codes for addresses, invoices, PSBTs, and wallet backups. Images are processed on the device and never stored or sent.
  • NFC: reads and writes PSBTs and keys with NFC tags and hardware signers.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: optionally unlocks the App. Biometric data is handled by iOS and never accessible to the App.

Each permission is optional and requested only when you use the feature.

5. App stores

If you download the App from the Apple App Store, Google Play, Zapstore, or another store, that store may collect information under its own privacy policy. App stores are not partners of satsigner, and we are not responsible for their practices.

6. Support requests

If you contact us by email, GitHub, or Nostr, we use what you send only to answer you. Email to satsigner.com is handled by our own mail server. It is not hosted by a third-party email provider, and connections to it are encrypted in transit (TLS). For end-to-end encrypted contact, message satsigner@satsigner.com on Nostr. Never send your seed phrase or private keys to anyone, including us.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no information from anyone.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@satsigner.com.
